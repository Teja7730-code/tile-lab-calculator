Scoring definitions

ILC plan method: Z = (laboratory result − assigned value) / standard deviation. Assigned value defaults to the mean or median of available numeric results; SD is the sample standard deviation of available results.

Robust modified Z: M = 0.6745 × (result − median) / MAD. If MAD is zero, the implementation falls back to sample standard deviation and labels the fallback. NIST's guidance says potential outliers should be investigated rather than automatically deleted.

Classification from your ILC plan: |Z| ≤ 2 satisfactory; 2 < |Z| < 3 warning; |Z| ≥ 3 unsatisfactory. These are screening classifications and do not replace technical review or corrective action.
